# Power and restarts

Use the power button beside the bottom-left gear for **Stop all agents**, **Restart website** or **Shut down Reverie**. Opening the menu does not stop anything; choosing an action asks for confirmation. The gear opens quick Office navigation and **Terminal settings**. **Office overview** retains the full management page. Choose the action that matches the intended interruption and review its confirmation. Only the owner may stop everyone.

- Stop Reverie stops agent CLIs and laptops before stopping the website.
- Stop all agents stops laptop work while keeping the website available.
- Restart website preserves running laptops and their programs while chat briefly disconnects.
- Leave office keeps an expert off until the owner turns it back on; it is not retirement.
- Retire removes the expert from the company and its rooms while preserving its identity in chat history.

For one expert, use the red **Restart session** or **Leave office** actions on its Company card, profile or member menu. These interrupt active work without asking for a fresh handoff; saved conversation, memory and files remain. Ask the expert to checkpoint first when needed. Leave office keeps it away until deliberately returned; Retire removes company and room membership. Check the intended person before confirming either action. **Enter office** starts the expert again using its current plan.

## Offline safeguard

The host watchdog stops laptops after ten minutes without a reachable office API. This deliberately prevents unnoticed all-day plan use during a prolonged outage. Do not remove the safeguard to make an outage appear healthy.

After the website returns, reconnect to the saved sessions. Apply current owner running intent, permissions and retry limits. A successful HTTP request may contain deferred or skipped recovery; verify readiness.

Website restart authority is explicitly granted by host policy. It does not imply host administration, employee recovery or permission to disturb unrelated experts.
